Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025. This page explains exactly what data PDF Experts does and does not process.

1. Our core commitment

PDF Experts is a fully static website. We do not run a backend, we do not have a database and we do not have any way to receive, store, read or analyse the documents you open in our tools. Every file you add is loaded directly into your browser's memory and processed there by open-source JavaScript libraries.

2. Files you open

Your PDFs and images never leave your device. We do not upload them to any server, and we do not store copies of them anywhere except in your browser's own memory for the duration of your session. When you close or reload the page, that memory is released by your browser like any other tab.

3. Cookies and local storage

We use a small amount of local storage — not traditional cookies — for two things:

  • Theme preference: whether you chose light or dark mode, so the next page you open matches your preference.
  • Cookie consent choice: whether you accepted or declined the consent banner.

These are stored on your device only, are not transmitted to us and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

4. Advertising

This site may display third-party advertising in the future. Advertising partners such as Google AdSense may use cookies to show personalised ads and to measure their effectiveness. You can control personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings and through your browser's cookie preferences. Advertisers do not receive any access to the contents of your files because those files never leave your browser.

5. Fonts and third-party libraries

To render the site we load:

  • The Inter font from Google Fonts. Google may log basic request data such as your IP address and user agent.
  • pdf-lib, pdf.js and JSZip from jsDelivr. jsDelivr may log basic request data.

These services do not receive any of your document contents.

6. Analytics

If we use analytics, we prefer privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics that only aggregate anonymous traffic counts. We will never enable analytics that link your visit to a personal identity.

7. Your rights

Because we do not store personal data or files, there is generally nothing for us to delete on request. You can remove the small amount of data we do store (theme and cookie choice) by clearing your browser's local storage for this site.

8. Children

PDF Experts is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the site. If the changes are significant we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please email hello@pdfexperts.site or use the contact page.
