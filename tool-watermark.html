      Professional watermarks without the risk

      A watermark tells a reader something important about a document: it is a draft, it is confidential, or it belongs to a specific reviewer. The Watermark tool draws a real text layer on every page — selectable, searchable and consistent — rather than pasting an image.

      Use opacity between 0.15 and 0.35 for a subtle mark that doesn't interfere with the content. Larger text works better for tall "CONFIDENTIAL" marks. The tiled pattern is useful for statement pages, exam papers and internal decks where you want every corner protected.

      Best practices

      • Keep the watermark text short — two or three words read best.
      • Contrast helps: dark red, black or navy at low opacity look professional.
      • Watermarks are a deterrent, not a security measure. For real protection use Protect PDF (coming soon).
      • Pair with Page numbers for reviewer-ready output.
