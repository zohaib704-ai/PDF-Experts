      Why convert images to PDF?

      PDFs are the safest way to send images to a client, a printer or a portfolio portal. Unlike a ZIP of photos, a PDF is guaranteed to open consistently everywhere and preserves the order of pages. This tool embeds each JPG or PNG directly into a new PDF page, without recompressing the image, so quality is preserved exactly.

      Choose A4 or US Letter for a print-ready document, or "Match image size" when every page should be exactly the image dimensions. Auto orientation reads each image's width and height, so a mixed album stays visually consistent — a phone photo and a landscape screenshot both look correct on their own pages.

      Recommendations

      • Reach for auto orientation when combining portrait and landscape photos.
      • Use A4 with portrait orientation for scanned documents.
      • For scanned PDFs that need OCR later, use one image per page (already the default).
      • To go the other way, try PDF to JPG.
