      Crop PDF margins cleanly

      Cropping removes visual whitespace without touching the content. The Crop PDF tool updates each page's CropBox and MediaBox — the two boxes that determine what a PDF reader and printer consider the page. This is the standard, lossless way to crop a PDF: no rasterisation, no re-encoding, no quality loss.

      Useful when a scanner adds a large border, when you want to remove headers and footers, or when you need a document that fills the page edge-to-edge. All four edges can be cropped independently, in millimetres, inches or points — the same units printers and PDF tools use.

      Tips

      • Preview the result in your PDF reader before sending it on.
      • Some documents hide text in the margin area. If you need to keep it, choose smaller crop values.
      • Combine cropping with Page numbers after cropping, not before.
