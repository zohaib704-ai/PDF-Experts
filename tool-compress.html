      How compression works

      This tool renders each PDF page as a JPEG image at your chosen quality and scale, then builds a new PDF from those images. On scanned, photo-heavy PDFs this can shrink files dramatically — often by 70-90%. On text-heavy PDFs the result may be similar in size or even larger, since text becomes images.

      Because compression is lossy and strips searchable text, always keep the original. Use compression for sharing, not for the copy you archive.

      When to use it

      • Emailing a large PDF that exceeds your provider's limit.
      • Uploading a PDF to a portal with a size cap.
      • Sharing a scan when you don't need searchable text.

      For text-heavy PDFs that need to stay searchable, try Split PDF to break the file into smaller parts instead.
