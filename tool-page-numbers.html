      Why add page numbers?

      Page numbers turn a stack of pages into a navigable document. When your PDF is sent to a printer, a client or a court, "see page 12" only works if page 12 is labelled. PDF Experts stamps the numbers as real, selectable text so they survive search, copy-paste and screen readers.

      Choose between simple numbers, "Page N of M", or an en-dash style that blends into printed books. Starting number lets you continue numbering from a previous document — great when you are building a single report from multiple PDFs, using Merge PDF first.

      Recommendations

      • Use font size 10–12 for A4 and Letter pages.
      • Bottom-center is the safest position for printing.
      • Bottom-right is common for reports and legal documents.
      • If your pages already have numbers, use "Start at" to avoid duplicates.
