Why PDF Experts

Built around your privacy.

Most PDF sites upload your files to a server. We do the opposite: processing happens in the browser tab you already have open.

Files stay local

Your PDF is processed on your own device. It is never uploaded, stored or scanned by us.

Fast, no queue

No upload time, no waiting for a server and no daily processing limit. The browser does the work.

Free to use

Every ready tool is free. No account, no watermark on your output and no hidden export fee.

Works everywhere

Use a desktop, tablet or phone. The interface adapts and the tools run in modern browsers.

How it works

Three steps. No uploads.

Choose a tool

Pick the task you need from the library. Ready tools open instantly; coming-soon tools are clearly labelled.

Add your file

Drag and drop a PDF or image, or browse from your device. Your file is read locally by the browser.

Download the result

Adjust the options, process the file and download the output. Nothing is sent to a server at any point.
